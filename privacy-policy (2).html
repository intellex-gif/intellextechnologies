Privacy Policy

How Intellex Technologies handles information in our mobile games and apps, including our block puzzle game.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games and applications published by Intellex Technologies (“we”, “us”, “our”), including our block puzzle game available on Google Play (the “App”). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at khurramshahbaz66@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We aim to collect as little information as possible. Depending on the version of the App you use, the following may be collected:

  • Gameplay data stored on your device, such as your score, level progress, settings and preferences. This stays on your device unless the App states otherwise.
  • Device and usage information collected automatically by third-party services we use, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash logs, general performance data and how the App is used.
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) used by advertising partners to show and measure ads.
  • Information you send us, for example when you email us for support. This includes your email address and the contents of your message.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect your name, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or files through the App.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the App;
  • To understand how the App is used and to fix bugs and improve performance;
  • To show advertisements and measure their performance, where ads are enabled;
  • To respond to support requests;
  • To protect the App against fraud, abuse and security issues, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

The App may use third-party services that collect information under their own privacy policies. These may include:

  • Google Play Services — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Firebase / Google Analytics (analytics and crash reporting) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising partners may use advertising identifiers to show personalised or non-personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers described above, when required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

6. Data retention

Gameplay data stored on your device remains there until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it. Data held by third-party services is retained according to their own policies. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet legal obligations.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at khurramshahbaz66@gmail.com and we will take steps to delete it.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can reset your advertising ID or limit ad personalisation in your device settings.
  • You can delete locally stored game data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. Contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
